🛡️ 100% Capital Safety

Every rented machine is backed by our full 30-day capital guarantee. When your lease term completes, your full rental amount is returned to your account.

01

Information We Collect

We only collect the basic details necessary to set up your mining account, credit your daily income, and send your withdrawals:

  • Account Details: Your username and email address.
  • Phone Number: Used for M-Pesa STK prompts, payment verifications, and WhatsApp support.
  • Payment Information: M-Pesa transaction reference codes submitted for manual Paybill approvals.
02

How We Use Your Data

Your details are used solely to deliver your mining experience:

  • To activate your chosen mining machine immediately upon rental.
  • To calculate and deposit your daily earnings every 24 hours.
  • To process your withdrawal requests straight to your M-Pesa or Bank account.
  • We never sell or share your personal data with any third-party marketing companies.
03

100% Capital Refund Guarantee

We operate on a transparent fixed-term rental model:

✓ 30-Day Complete Cycle:

During the 30-day lease duration, you receive daily automated payouts. At the end of day 30, your 100% initial rent capital is refunded back into your available balance, ready to withdraw or re-rent.

04

Account & Password Security

All user accounts are protected with industry-standard bcrypt encryption and SSL connection certificates. Please keep your password safe and never share your credentials with anyone.

05

M-Pesa & Payment Protection

When paying via M-Pesa STK Prompt or manual Paybill within your account, all payments are securely processed and verified with Safaricom to ensure immediate hardware lease activation and credit to your account.
